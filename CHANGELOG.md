# 1.0.0

Create books with PNG illustrations up to 4096 × 4096 and editable text.

Craft an Illustrated Book from a book, feather and ink sac. Right-click it to open sixteen editable pages. Each page can contain a PNG and up to 2048 text characters. Drag a PNG onto the screen or enter its local path and select Upload PNG. Text is saved automatically when changing pages or closing the book. PNG dimensions may be up to 4096 pixels on either side, with files up to 72 MiB. Images are verified and stored in the server world, then sent to other players when they read the book. Keep the illustratedbooks folder when copying a world. Books carry their page text and image references as item data. Texture caching is limited to 96 MiB and smaller GPU texture limits are handled by downsampling.
